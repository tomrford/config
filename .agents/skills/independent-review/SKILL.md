---
name: independent-review
description: Guidelines for a competent and valuable independent review.
---

Some tips for a good independent review experience:

- Always use fresh context rather than forking to avoid poisoning its context with your own biases.
- Avoiding priming the subagent; "is my approach good?" -> "what do you think of this approach?". Models are inherently biased to take your side.
- Given the above bias, suggest the work could be improved/is not perfect, to encourage it to look for potential issues.
- Ask it what follow-on work exists; it's likely that a second pass over some parts could consolidate code or further simplify.
